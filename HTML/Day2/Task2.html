"Patience"

Once upon there was a school called PEMS. Every class in that school had 3 sections A,B,C. But 10th class was special because it had 2 sections A,B. The school academic year starts from June. 10th class was special because it had a special class called A+ which is hidden class which starts from November which can only be attended by people who have cleared with flying grades in mid term exams.

This is story of boy called Zar. He was introverted, childish and naive. Not only that he had anger issues we couldn't control his anger which came along with OCD. And he was youngest in his class so he was immature compared to others. Still he was Geniune,Honest,Loyal,Clever,Studious,Respectful. He was in section A in 10th class. In same there was person called Jeyat. Total opposite of Zar, he was clever,strong but was Disrespectful, Cunning, Pervert. He used to speak bad words about teachers behind their backs and used to look at girls with creepy looks. In was class seat allotment day the worst thing happened was zar was going to seat beside Jeyat. Zar used to do his usual everyday things but one day he heard Jeyat speaking bad about teachers and looking out for girls. Zar called him out but things actually got worse Jeyat started bullying Zar Jeyat would throw Zar's bag on the floor which is big problem for person with OCD , used to hit Zar and once he even stabbed Zar with pen and spread rumors about Zar among teachers and would provoke Zar. Zar who was mature and already suffering with anger management issues used to retaliate only to get defeated as Jeyat was strong.

Things became worse when Zar's reputation came down rapidly and teachers started to see him as delinquent and one teacher even said Zar was worst student he had ever taught .When Zar was suffering he realized that he is immature and honest and weak so he can't do anything about that one but he is clever so he must use his brain . From next day whenever Jeyat tried to do something he started controlling himself, started staying calm as water and do is work even though he was getting provoked. Mid exams were done and it was announcement day of students who were gaining access to A+ section. Zar was selected among them were Jeyat wasn't and Zar switched section from then he totally cut ties with Jeyant wouldn't respond to him and wouldn't even talk to him. He continued this for months. Now there are no fights Zar is well in new class and teacher who once criticized Zar now told that Zar has changed so much whereas that Jeyat is still like trash and praised Zar.By time Zar graduated his reputation was slightly repaired and he left school for next step in his career.

"Just stay patient lots of people will try to sabotage you and they will pay for it by themselves."
